Assumptions, sources & limitations
  • Sources: the shared 40-centre application catalogue supplies centre codes, names and states. Each centre has its own repeatable synthetic daily volume, starting backlog and hourly arrival pattern, seeded by its centre code within state-level workload ranges. These are not measured demand. Processing-capacity assumptions are shared across centres. No merchant, manifest, historical demand, weather or roster feeds are connected. No measured forecast accuracy or confidence is claimed.
  • Typical weekday volume: {{ '{:,}'.format(facility.daily_volume) }} parcels, adjusted by fixed weekday factors. Existing processing backlog: {{ facility.initial_backlog }} parcels at sorting. All assumed volumes are eligible for processing; held/inspection parcels are outside this model.
  • Demo manifest split is 70% confirmed / 30% forecast. These are not real bookings. Expected counts are rounded to whole parcels.
  • Hours: 06:00-22:00 daily. Unload 1,500/hour; induct 1,400/hour; sort limited by 12 staff at 120 effort units/hour and two lanes at 750 units/hour; load 1,300/hour. No floor/storage capacity is inferred.
  • Each stage consumes at least one hourly step, with FIFO queues. Cutoffs at 18:00 and 22:00; arrivals are assigned the first connection at least four hours after their original scheduled arrival. Later arrivals are due at 18:00 next day. Cutoff misses count each parcel once.
  • Transfers are synthetic authorised receiving reservations, max 800/day and 400/hour, with four hours allowed for transport and receiving processing. Only eligible unload-stage parcels with sufficient connection time can move. Receiving completion is assumed, not simulated.
  • Cost excludes base labour, vehicles, penalties and recovery beyond the horizon. Additional sorters cost AUD 55/hour from 14:00; extended facility hours cost AUD 840/hour plus extra sorters; transfers cost AUD 2/parcel.
  • This is a processing-centre demonstration, not route, delivery-round, network optimisation or an approved operating plan. Scenarios are request-scoped and not persisted.
