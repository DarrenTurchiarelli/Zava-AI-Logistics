Assumptions, sources & limitations
  • Sources: the shared catalogue supplies centre identities. When both parcel and approval feeds are complete, distinct eligible application records supply the starting queues. Unknown current locations, conflicting identities, unsupported statuses, held parcels and unresolved approval review are excluded and reported. Origin/lodgement location and destination do not establish current occupancy. Application data can include seeded demo records.
  • Future arrivals use repeatable centre-code-seeded daily volumes and hourly patterns. Typical weekday volume: {{ '{:,}'.format(facility.daily_volume) }} parcels, adjusted by fixed weekday factors. Future arrivals are additional synthetic workload, not a second count of the application snapshot or confirmed manifest bookings. No measured forecast accuracy or confidence is claimed.
  • Starting workload: {{ facility.initial_backlog }} parcels, {{ 'from the application snapshot at the indicated stages' if operations and operations.connected else 'synthetic and placed at sorting because current eligibility could not be established' }}. All deadlines remain assumed; no timestamps are interpreted as confirmed arrivals. Held and pending-review items are never released by the planner.
  • Only today's active delivery manifests linked by parcel identity supply outbound context. Older/differently dated manifests are excluded. Driver-directory accounts are not an availability roster, and delivery capacity is not sorting capacity. No inbound booking, weather, trained-sorter roster or measured throughput feed is connected.
  • Hours: 06:00-22:00 daily. Unload 1,500/hour; induct 1,400/hour; sort limited by 12 staff at 120 effort units/hour and two lanes at 750 units/hour; load 1,300/hour. No floor/storage capacity is inferred.
  • Each stage consumes at least one hourly step, with FIFO queues. Cutoffs at 18:00 and 22:00; arrivals are assigned the first connection at least four hours after their original scheduled arrival. Later arrivals are due at 18:00 next day. Cutoff misses count each parcel once.
  • Transfers are synthetic authorised receiving reservations, max 800/day and 400/hour, with four hours allowed for transport and receiving processing. Only eligible unload-stage parcels with sufficient connection time can move. Receiving completion is assumed, not simulated.
  • Costs use your editable illustrative rates, not verified market quotes. Normal site labour is paid 06:00-22:00, regardless of absence; site overhead applies every elapsed hour. Costed site-team sizes do not change processing capacity. Extra sorters are paid from 14:00 until closing; extended-hours crew pay is additional after 22:00. Rates are blended, without automatic award, weekend, public-holiday or shift calculations. Actual modelled transfers incur handling plus whole transport loads rounded up per local day. Restoring an unavailable lane incurs one fee per horizon, not per day. All figures use one GST-exclusive basis. Base fleet, claims, service penalties, revenue loss and post-horizon recovery costs are excluded; transfer transport is only an allowance, not a route quote.
  • This is a processing-centre demonstration, not route, delivery-round, network optimisation or an approved operating plan. Scenarios are request-scoped and not persisted.
